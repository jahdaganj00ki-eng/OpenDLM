OpenDLM is set up

A free download manager for Windows. No trial, no licence key, nothing to buy.

What this extension does

  • Hands downloads you start to OpenDLM, which splits them across several connections.
  • Adds OpenDLM entries to the right-click menu, including on selected links and on a download the browser has already begun.
  • Offers to grab detected video and audio.

If nothing happens

  • OpenDLM must be running; this extension hands the work over to it.
  • Hold Shift while clicking a link to keep it in the browser, or Alt to force it into OpenDLM.
  • Check the desktop app under Options -> Browser.

Your settings stay on this machine

  • Nothing is sent anywhere except to the OpenDLM application on this computer.
